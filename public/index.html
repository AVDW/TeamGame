How it works

You'll be split into secret teams. Each team drives one sprite up the screen. You'll each control just one direction (we'll show you which) — but you're not told which sprite is yours. Press your button, watch the board to spot your sprite, and coordinate with your team to weave past the traps. First team to the top wins. 🏆
Anyone can start once everyone's in. Teams are formed automatically — the more players, the more sprites to race.
